Introduction

AI in education has been a controversial debate since the beginning of generative AI. Some believe that it is a helpful tool, that can help aid students who fall behind or have difficulties learning. While some believe that its cons outweigh the pros, and that we can become to over reliant on ai causing it to be detrimental to our learning.

Many students use AI alongside their learning for a variety of different reasons, but some may use it to finish coursework for them causing them to not learn what is required. Many teachers and lecturers also use it to help with tasks and increase efficiency. However, overuse of AI in learning materials can cause the overall quality to decrease.

Accessibility and Individual Learning

One of the positives that AI has is how it can help students with disadvantages and can be considered accessible to people that are struggling. There are many different scenarios that using ai might benefit someone who is struggling, such as if someone’s literary skills are behind, they can use AI to make it easier for them to understand the text.

It can also help someone who has trouble understanding the teaching styles of the course be told in a way that they understand and be tailored to how they like to learn.

Rob Gibson says that advancements in ai allow people with disabilities the same access to resources for students without disabilitiesi. [10] I believe that ai does have a lot of help in areas that people with disabilities as it can explain things in a very specific way if the user requests it to, which can help people understand topics easier.

Supporting Teachers and Lecturers

Ai can also be used to help lecturers and teachers focus more on educating rather than administrative and IT tasks that may draw focus away from teaching. This can include creating presentations or documents for students, which can be time consuming.

But with ai you can create those exceptionally quicker for students and allow you to focus on teaching them. Ai can also help with feedback. Due to teachers having many students, getting through feedback from them can be tiring and time consuming.

With ai you can summarise the feedback from students. This can help lecturers understand concerns and worries from students and help make their courses better.

Seating Plan Blog says that ai thrives on data, meaning that it is perfect for assessment scores, behavioural patterns and student preferences.ii [22] This agrees with me in saying that ai can be used to make the IT and feedback process more efficient so that the teacher can focus on learning materials.

Feedback and Engagement

AI can also offer 24/7 instant feedback on work for students. If a student has stayed up at night or during the weekend, and would like feedback on work they made, they can give it over to AI to give feedback on their submitted work.

This means that students no longer need to wait for their teachers or lecturers to check through all their work to give feedback. It also means that lecturers and teachers no longer must go through sometimes hundreds of pieces of work to give everyone feedback, which can be mentally draining and tiring for them.

This decreases the time students must wait to receive feedback and allows them to make changes that is needed for their assignment. One article says, “AI-generated feedback can function as a credible component of formative assessment in higher education.”iii. [12]

AI can also increase engagement for students. As it can be more tailored towards students, they can ask the AI to tell the topic in a variety of different ways that help the student understand it. Additionally, they can also ask the AI for quizzes that help them understand different topics and ideas in the course.
