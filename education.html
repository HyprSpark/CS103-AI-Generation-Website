Introduction

AI in education has been a controversial debate since the beginning of generative AI. Some believe that it is a helpful tool, that can help aid students who fall behind or have difficulties learning. While some believe that its cons outweigh the pros, and that we can become too over-reliant on AI causing it to be detrimental to our learning.

Many students use AI alongside their learning for a variety of different reasons, but some may use it to finish coursework for them causing them to not learn what is required. Many teachers and lecturers also use it to help with tasks and increase efficiency. However, overuse of AI in learning materials can cause the overall quality to decrease.

AI is used by many students for completing tasks like homework, writing essays, and preparing assignments. AI can be used by teachers for planning the lessons and marking the students' work. But there are various bad impacts of the usage of AI on the educational system. AI may cause dependency on technology and prevent the learning of certain essential skills from the students.

Accessibility and Individual Learning

One of the positives that AI has is how it can help students with disadvantages and can be considered accessible to people that are struggling. There are many different scenarios that using AI might benefit someone who is struggling, such as if someone’s literary skills are behind, they can use AI to make it easier for them to understand the text.

It can also help someone who has trouble understanding the teaching styles of the course be told in a way that they understand and be tailored to how they like to learn.

Rob Gibson says that advancements in AI allow people with disabilities the same access to resources for students without disabilities. [10] I believe that AI does have a lot of help in areas that people with disabilities face, as it can explain things in a very specific way if the user requests it to, which can help people understand topics easier.

Not always AI is a positive tool to have in our lives. There is a great chance that the use of AI in education will lead to the reliance of the students on it. They will not try to come up with answers on their own but will expect AI to do this for them. For instance, a student will use AI to write an essay instead of looking into the topic and coming up with the answers himself. It will help him to save his time, but this way he will not be able to benefit from it. Students should think, train, and solve problems to develop their knowledge. Without doing this, they will not learn anything from it, and it will be hard for them to do something else when they do not have access to AI in exams or somewhere else.

Supporting Teachers and Lecturers

AI can also be used to help lecturers and teachers focus more on educating rather than administrative and IT tasks that may draw focus away from teaching. This can include creating presentations or documents for students, which can be time-consuming.

But with AI you can create those exceptionally quicker for students and allow you to focus on teaching them. AI can also help with feedback. Due to teachers having many students, getting through feedback from them can be tiring and time-consuming.

With AI you can summarise the feedback from students. This can help lecturers understand concerns and worries from students and help make their courses better.

Seating Plan Blog says that AI thrives on data, meaning that it is perfect for assessment scores, behavioural patterns and student preferences. [20] This agrees with me in saying that AI can be used to make the IT and feedback process more efficient so that the teacher can focus on learning materials.

With all the positive sides of AI in education, there are also some drawbacks. A drawback of AI is that it promotes cheating. Students have the opportunity to use AI in order to write an essay, give answers to questions and finish an assignment. They will submit this work under their name, but they did not put efforts in it and told nothing about it to their teachers. This is a dishonest approach toward other students, who take efforts to do their work honestly. The thing is that cheating does not give teachers the real picture of students' knowledge. For example, a student gets high grades thanks to AI, but not due to his or her own abilities and efforts. So, there is no real information in the grades.

Feedback and Engagement

AI can also offer 24/7 instant feedback on work for students. If a student has stayed up at night or during the weekend, and would like feedback on work they made, they can give it over to AI to give feedback on their submitted work.

This means that students no longer need to wait for their teachers or lecturers to check through all their work to give feedback. It also means that lecturers and teachers no longer must go through sometimes hundreds of pieces of work to give everyone feedback, which can be mentally draining and tiring for them.

This decreases the time students must wait to receive feedback and allows them to make changes that is needed for their assignment. One article says, “AI-generated feedback can function as a credible component of formative assessment in higher education.”. [11]

AI can also increase engagement for students. As it can be more tailored towards students, they can ask the AI to tell the topic in a variety of different ways that help the student understand it. Additionally, they can also ask the AI for quizzes that help them understand different topics and ideas in the course.

These quizzes can then adapt to the student’s skill level, meaning that they can make it as easy or as difficult as they’d like. This allows the student to then slowly ramp up in difficulty as they learn more about the course. All these together helps students stay engaged with the course as they can tailor it to how they want to learn which can make students enjoy a course more. Euneos says, “When students feel they have agency in their learning process, their internal drive typically increases.” [6]

AI tools do not always give correct answers. Sometimes, they provide information that is false, unclear, or out of date. Students who trust these answers without checking them may learn the wrong facts. This can lead to mistakes in assignments, exams, and other schoolwork. For example, AI may give an incorrect explanation of a scientific topic or include false details in a history essay. Students who do not have enough knowledge may find it difficult to notice these errors. This makes learning more difficult because students may believe that they understand a subject when they have actually learned incorrect information. Teachers and students must spend extra time checking AI answers to make sure they are reliable.

Individual Learning

AI can also help encourage individual learning for students. This is because AI is a personalised process and can encourage students to go off and learn the things they don’t understand by themselves. Students can research online the things that they are confused about. Then use AI to summarise, simplify or explain things that they find online. This then encourages students to research things that they do not understand and helps with individual learning and not always rely on a teacher. Abbie Forbus Everett says that using AI to support personalised learning can help improve efficiency and the effectiveness of research. [12]

When an individual is using an AI tool to learn something, AI can use critical thinking to give the best possible answers. Critical thinking involves the examination, questioning, and evaluation of the information for accuracy. Such qualities are essential in education since learners are supposed to learn and form opinions on the concepts taught to them. The learners that will use artificial intelligence for all their assignments may lose the capacity to critically analyse their work since they will not verify the information presented by the AI systems. Learners will not search for information from other sources like textbooks. This may hinder the development of their critical skills, which are necessary in education.
