Planning & Intermediate Milestones

To ensure our website was completed in good time, we had to make sure to evenly distribute our roles and to set out milestone targets to keep on top of work so that we were able to move onto the next part of developing our website. Initially, we were hoping to have our individual content and webpages complete by the 12th of October, which we went slightly over by a couple of days but still had finished within plenty of time to allow us to proofread and put it all together into the website's final state.

Since the milestone and planning stage at the start of developing our website, we also decided that we would each take on a higher word count to allow other members of the team to write less and focus more on the development of the website itself. We found this extremely effective in improving the quality of our website, given the previous experience that some of us had working with HTML and CSS already.
