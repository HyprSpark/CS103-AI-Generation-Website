AI in Education

AI in education has been a controversial debate since the beginning of generative AI. Some believe that it is a helpful tool, that can help aid students who fall behind or have difficulties learning. While some believe that its cons outweigh the pros, and that we can become to over reliant on ai causing it to be detrimental to our learning.
